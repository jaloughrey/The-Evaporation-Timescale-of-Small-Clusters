# Evaporation Timescale of Small Clusters
### Introduction
Stellar clusters describe a group of gravitationally bound stars; they are commonly categorised into two distinct types. Open clusters are young and loosely bound groups containing tens to thousands of stars. The much larger globular clusters contain anywhere from tens of thousands to millions of tightly bound stars and display some of the oldest stellar populations in the universe. The vast majority of stars appear within these clusters; hence it is inevitable that these systems have become a critical pathway into developing our understanding of galaxies and the wider universe. 
<br>
<br>
This program aims to provide an easy-to-use tool that will create fully customisable star cluster simulations based on a Hermite 4th order integration scheme. As well as provide a number of analysis tools for different research applications. Here is a research project that utilises this program to explore evaporation in small open clusters. The paper also has a detailed methodology explaining the Hermite scheme implementation and the physics used in defining clusters.
